About Us

Giovani's by the Sea was born from a love of classic Neapolitan technique and Cape Town's beautiful coastline. We source the best tomatoes and cheeses from Italy, pair them with local seafood and produce, and bake every pizza in a 400°C wood-fired oven. Our dining room is designed for relaxed, classy coastal dining — perfect for family meals, date nights, and celebrations.
